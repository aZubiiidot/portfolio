PROJECT DESCRIPTION
  • Role: 3D Generalist
  • Core Stack: Maya, Redshift, Fusion Studio
  • Main Focus: CAD Model Clean-up, Shading and Lookdev, Basic Compositing

A short online marketing video for a medical technology device.
The workflow relied on Maya, Redshift, and Fusion Studio. My primary responsibility was transforming high-density engineering CAD models into optimized, render-ready production assets, handling clean-up, lookdev, and minor compositing support.

MY TASKS
  • CAD Model Clean-up: I ingested raw engineering models into Maya, cleaning up heavy geometry, resolving shading artifacts, and remodeling specific parts where necessary to ensure artifact-free rendering.
  • Shading & Lookdevelopment: I created clean, realistic material networks inside Redshift to accurately represent the device's physical surfaces and internal components.
  • Compositing Support: I assisted in the final post-production phase inside Fusion Studio, helping to blend the 3D renders with graphic overlays and text elements for the marketing delivery.
